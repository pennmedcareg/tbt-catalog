Accreditation
One-page CoC & NAPBC Corrective Action Plan (CAP) guide: a CAP must explain what went wrong, why, how it was fixed, and how it stays fixed; distinguishes a CAP (demonstrates the deficiency was fixed and prevented going forward) from an Appeal (argues compliance already existed at the time of review — corrective actions completed after the site visit cannot be used to justify an appeal)
